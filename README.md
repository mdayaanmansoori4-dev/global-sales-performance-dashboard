# 📊 Global Sales Performance Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing global sales performance from FY2011–2016 across 5 countries and 3 product categories (Bikes, Accessories, Clothing).
<img width="1088" height="807" alt="Dashboard_preview" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3e4644db-7549-45eb-b01a-b709ae775940" />
## What's Inside
- **112,000+ rows** of cleaned transactional sales data
- **Star schema** data model with a proper date dimension table
- **15+ DAX measures** — Total Revenue, Profit Margin %, YoY Growth, MTD, Top-N Ranking, and more
- **5 interactive slicers** — Date, Country, Product Category, Age Group, Gender
- **7 visuals** — KPI cards, trend line, bar charts, donut chart, table, and a revenue/margin combo chart
- **Custom theme** — consistent color palette and layout across every visual

## Key Business Insights

- **Revenue & Profitability:** Generated approximately $85M in total revenue and $32M in total profit, with a displayed profit margin of 37.8%.
- **Product Category Performance:** Bikes contributed approximately $61M (72.42%) of total revenue, followed by Accessories at $15M (17.71%) and Clothing at $8M (9.87%).
- **Geographic Performance:** The United States recorded the highest revenue among the countries shown in the dashboard.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Adults aged 35–64 generated the highest revenue among the age groups shown.
- **Time-Based Analysis:** Monthly revenue trends and yearly revenue/profit-margin comparisons help explore performance changes over FY2011–2016.

## Business Value

This dashboard helps users compare product categories, evaluate country-level sales, analyze customer segments, and monitor revenue and profitability trends.

Power BI Desktop · DAX · Power Query · Python (data cleaning)
